The Aelf-King Sagas

by S.C. Kincaid

The Aelf-King Sagas is a historical fantasy trilogy set in a Europe half remembered and half invented, where the oldest powers of winter still move behind the stories people tell. It follows the making of a legend across centuries, back through the folklore and myth that shaped it and forward through the man the world would come to call Santa Claus. History is the ground here, and the fantasy grows out of it.

About the Press

The name holds two ideas. Amber is old light carried whole across centuries, sap turned to stone with a single moment still sealed inside it. Roots are the half of a tree's life that happens unseen, the work below the ground that decides whether anything above it can stand. Amberroot Press is the meeting of the two, old light and buried work, brought up into books.

Founded in 2026, Amberroot Press is independent. It publishes in print, ebook, and audio, with distribution through the trade and direct to readers.

The Amberroot Pledge

How this press conducts itself, in writing, permanently.

  1. We never charge an author we publish, and we never publish an author we charge. The services line and any future imprint acquisitions are permanently separate.
  2. Client books carry the client's imprint, never ours. We install; you own.
  3. No royalty share, no revenue share, no percentage of anything. Fixed prices, published on this page.
  4. We show our own numbers. The case study for every service is our own catalog, documented before we sold anything.
  5. We say no. If a manuscript or a timeline is not ready for a launch, the audit says so in writing, and we do not sell the next step.
